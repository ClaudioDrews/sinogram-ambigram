04 / research hypotheses

Local compensation and even harmonic fields

Two mathematical sketches of spatial thickness fields, not outputs of the R-ODD or harmonic construction algorithms. They illustrate the difference between a localized adjustment and a field deliberately invariant under 180° rotation.

0.65

Local response (concept only)

A single localized Gaussian patch: not necessarily C₂ symmetric, and not the R-ODD operator.

C₂-even harmonic field (concept only)

An even cosine-product field, f(−x,−y)=f(x,y). Not an optimized stroke result.

PRs #14 (R-ODD) and #15 (harmonic) are separate, unmerged experiments. This visualization demonstrates an underlying distinction without claiming that either operator was experimentally superior.

05 / perception

A geometric match is a candidate, not a readable ambigram

There are three separate observations: source shapes can be close under half-turn, a constructor can change the drawing, and human readers may or may not identify both intended characters. The measurements on this page demonstrate geometry; they never substitute for a blinded reading study.
