Explore the measured effects of null-model normalisation and rotation-centre search. The current dataset uses Japanese standard kanji glyphs. Chinese PRC geometry is an independent workstream.

These are geometric candidates, not demonstrated ambigrams. No native-reader study has been completed. Labels show distances and ranking quantities, not probabilities that a human can read a drawing.

Methods and limitations

The null model compares each character against 30 randomly selected partners in its stroke-complexity stratum (seed 20261107). Rotation centres were searched on a 9 × 9 grid of offsets. Distances are recorded in separate _grid and _exact fields. Adjusting the centre can improve alignment even when the underlying drawing does not change.
