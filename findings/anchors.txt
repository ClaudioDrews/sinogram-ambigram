Purpose
-------
The decision rule in T1.0.3 asks whether the de-biased metric (per-glyph
z-score plus optimal rotation centre) still identifies the pairs that are
genuinely good. It does so with a fixed list of anchors, so that the answer
cannot be reached by choosing anchors after seeing the result.

Scale of the v1 values quoted below: EXACT point-cloud Hausdorff, normalised
by 109. Source: data/rotation_pairs.csv (5,180 refined rows).

B. DIAGNOSTIC SET (2) — reported, but NOT part of the binary rule
------------------------------------------------------------------

These two are reported in findings/debias-report.md but are deliberately
excluded from the pass/fail rule in section C. They are the cases the
independent review (CR3) identified as being penalised by the fixed rotation
centre:

  #  pair          v1 H (exact)  CR3 note
  8  十 ↔ 十       0.11498       centroid alignment reported as 0.115 → 0.070
  9  中 ↔ 中       0.10988       centroid alignment reported as 0.110 → 0.084

Why they are kept out of the rule: the rule in section C was fixed as a
pass/fail gate over 7 anchors, with the threshold "<= 3 of 7 in the top 5%".
Adding two anchors that the v1 metric ranks poorly would change the
denominator and the semantics of a threshold that was pre-registered before
the results existed. Reporting them separately keeps the rule's meaning
intact while still recording the evidence that the de-biasing works where it
is expected to.

Their interpretation is fixed in advance as well:
  - If both are promoted into the top 5% of their stratum, that is consistent
    with the optimal-centre stage doing what CR3 predicted.
  - If neither moves, that is evidence the optimal-centre search is not
    recovering the improvement CR3 measured, and it must be investigated
    before Gate A.
  - One moving and one not is a partial result, to be reported as such.


C. DECISION RULE (fixed before the results; D17)
--------------------------------------------------

Applied to the 7 anchors in section A, after z-score normalisation and the
optimal rotation centre:

  1. ESCALATED if fewer than 50% of the 7 anchors (i.e. 3 or fewer) are in
     the top 5% of their complexity stratum (percentile-z >= 95).
     Interpretation: suspicion that the distance model itself is at fault,
     not only the ranking.

  2. Any anchor outside the top 5% requires a written explanation in
     findings/debias-report.md. The explanation is approved by the
     maintainer, not by the agent (G11: no self-certification).

  3. Otherwise: GO.

Stratum definition: the rounded mean of the two characters' stroke counts.
Percentile: the rank of the pair's z within that stratum, over the full
unordered pair space including self-pairs.


D. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT CLAIM
---------------------------------

These anchors are pairs the geometry ranks highly. They are not established
as readable ambigrams. No native-reader validation has been published for
this project, and a high percentile-z says nothing about legibility. The
threshold in section C is a decision about whether the metric change
preserved what the geometry already found, not about perceptual validity.
